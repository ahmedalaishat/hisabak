Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 June 2026

Hisabak is a personal finance tracker for Android and iOS. It is built to keep your financial data on your device. We never collect, transmit, sell, or share your transactions, brands, categories, or budgets. The data that leaves your device is anonymous crash diagnostics and anonymous usage analytics — and, only if you switch it on yourself, the text of a bank message that none of your parsing templates recognise, so it can be read for you. Both are described below.

The short version: Hisabak has no accounts and no advertising. Everything you record stays on your phone. To help us fix bugs and understand which features are used, the app sends anonymous crash reports and anonymous usage analytics (which screens you open and which actions you take, in aggregate) through Google Firebase — and nothing else. These never include your transactions or any personal or financial data.

Data we collect

None of your financial data. Hisabak does not collect or transmit your transactions, brands, categories, budgets, or any personal information — those stay on your device. The only exceptions are anonymous, technical crash diagnostics, anonymous usage analytics, and two optional features that send data only at your request — one you turn on, one you tap each time; see Crash reporting, Usage analytics, Online parsing, and AI review below.

Crash reporting

To find and fix crashes, Hisabak uses Google Firebase Crashlytics. When the app crashes, it sends Google a diagnostic report containing:

  • the crash stack trace and the app version,
  • basic device information (model, operating-system version, language, and a randomly generated app-installation identifier).

These reports never include your transactions, brands, categories, budgets, SMS content, or any personal or financial information — only technical details about the crash. Google processes this data on our behalf as described in the Firebase Privacy and Security documentation. Crash reporting is active in the released app and is disabled in development builds.

Usage analytics

To understand which features people use and improve the app, Hisabak uses Google Firebase Analytics. It records anonymous, aggregate interaction events such as which screens you open and which actions you take (for example, that a transaction was added, or that onboarding finished).

These events never include your transactions, brands, categories, budgets, SMS content, note text, exact amounts, or any personal or financial information. Amounts, when relevant, are recorded only as coarse, non-identifying size ranges. Google processes this data on our behalf as described in the Firebase Privacy and Security documentation. Analytics is active in the released app and is disabled in development builds.

Where your data is stored

Your transactions, brands, categories, and budgets are stored only in a private database on your device, in app-private storage that Android protects with its built-in file-based encryption — other apps cannot read it, and the data on disk is encrypted, so it cannot be read if the device's storage is extracted. Automatic cloud backup is disabled, so this data is not copied off the device by the system. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Bank SMS

Some distributions of Hisabak (outside the Google Play version) can read incoming bank transaction SMS to automatically create a transaction. When this feature is present:

  • SMS messages are read and parsed locally on your device only, to extract the amount and merchant.
  • Message content is not transmitted off your device unless you turn on Parse with the online model yourself (Settings → SMS parsing). That setting is off by default.
  • The feature requires your explicit Android permission and can be left disabled.

Online parsing (optional, off by default)

Most phones cannot run an on-device AI model. If you turn on Parse with the online model, a message that none of your parsing templates recognise is sent to Hisabak's parsing service so its amount, merchant, and date can be read. This is the only feature that sends message content off your device, and it does nothing until you enable it.

  • Only unrecognised messages are sent. Anything a template already parses is handled entirely on your device and never transmitted.
  • Nothing is stored. The service does not log or retain message text; it reads the message, returns the fields, and keeps nothing.
  • Parsing sends nothing else — only the text of the message being parsed, plus the names of your existing brands so the merchant can be matched to one. Your transactions, categories, budgets, and balances are not part of it.
  • The message text is processed by Anthropic to perform the parsing, and is not used to train models.
  • Once a message is parsed, Hisabak learns a template from it, so future messages in that format are read on your device and are not sent.
  • Turning the setting off stops all sending immediately.

AI review (optional, only when you ask)

The insights screen shows a review of your period worked out on your phone. Beneath it you can tap Explain with AI to have a summary of the period sent to Hisabak's service so a model can explain what changed and suggest what to do. There is no setting to leave on: nothing is sent unless you tap, and each tap sends once. This is separate from online parsing.

  • Only totals are sent: your income and spending for the period and the one before it, your spending per category with its name and monthly limit, and the total and count of uncategorized spending, plus the currency and your app language.
  • Never sent: individual transactions, notes, brand or shop names, bank messages, account details, or anything that identifies you. The request has no field for them.
  • See what’s shared, next to the button, shows you the exact figures a tap would send.
  • Nothing is stored. The service does not log or retain the figures; it returns the explanation and keeps nothing. The figures are processed by Anthropic and are not used to train models.
  • The explanation is kept on your phone and shown again while your figures are exactly unchanged; nothing is sent for that. Any change to the figures asks you again.
  • Suggestions never change anything by themselves: tapping one opens the editor for you to confirm.

The Google Play version of Hisabak does not access SMS at all. In that version you add transactions by sharing a bank SMS to the app, selecting its text, or pasting it — all handled on-device.

Permissions we use

  • Notifications — to show you transaction confirmations and budget alerts.
  • Internet — used only to send the anonymous crash reports described above.
  • Receive SMS (non-Play builds only) — to auto-capture bank transaction alerts locally, as described above.

Children

Hisabak is intended for adults and is not directed at children.
